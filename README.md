**Technical Artist — Creature Rigging & Procedural Systems**

I specialize in procedural rigging and creature systems, with a focus on **Houdini and Maya**. My work combines rigging, procedural computation, and deformation to develop technical solutions for biological and mechanical characters.

### Houdini

`KineFX` · `APEX` · `VEX` · `OpenCL`

Developing procedural rigging and deformation systems using Houdini's KineFX and APEX frameworks, with VEX and OpenCL for custom geometry processing and parallel computation.

Areas of focus include:

* Procedural skeleton and rig construction
* KineFX character workflows
* APEX rig graphs
* VEX-based geometry and attribute processing
* OpenCL / GPU-parallel computation
* Procedural deformation and motion systems

### Maya

`Python` · `Rigging` · `Matrix Workflows` · `Deformation`

Building creature and mechanical rigs with an emphasis on reusable systems and pipeline-oriented tool development.

Current work includes custom Python tools for rig construction, joint processing, transform operations, rigging utilities, and workflow automation.

### R&D

My technical interests center on the relationship between **rigging, mathematics, mechanics, and procedural computation**.

Current research includes procedural creature deformation, biomechanical rigging systems, matrix-based transformations, spline-driven structures, GPU computation, and mathematical approaches to character motion.
